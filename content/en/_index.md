## About Me

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Sun Yat-sen University](https://cse.sysu.edu.cn/en), advised by Assoc. Prof. [Jiangsu Du](https://dujiangsu.github.io/) and Prof. [Zhiguang Chen](https://cse.sysu.edu.cn/teacher/ChenZhiguang). I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Sun Yat-sen University, where I was also a member of [SYSU-SCC](https://scc.sysu.tech/).

📌 My research interests lie in AI Infrastructure and Scalable Online and Offline Large-Scale LLM Serving Systems.

✉️ Welcome to contact me for any discussion and cooperation!

💥 💥 I am opening for internship!  If you are interested, please feel free to contact me.  💥 💥
